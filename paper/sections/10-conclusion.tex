\section{Conclusion}
The usual scalar-communication account of distributed ES is true under one
update placement but incomplete as a systems model. Across the measured GPU
and TPU grids, one host each on a fat fabric, locally contracting
population shards and all-reducing a
model-sized update wins every dense and seed-regenerated cell. Repeating the
contraction after a scalar gather returns as the better placement when
low-rank perturbations make the avoided work small. The sign map moves with
the accelerator and recurs, with both signs intact, on Qwen2.5-0.5B. The
low-rank rewrite's GPU-shaped motivation also strengthens rather than inverts
on the TPU. Centered-rank shaping adds one shared fitness gather followed by a
replicated local sort; the sort, rather than communication, dominates that
barrier at extreme populations.

The validation chain gives the systems result a measured quality boundary. An
approximately square-root alignment law from a synthetic block predicts
full-rank cosine with the true teacher-forced-NLL gradient within 10\% at 0.5B
and 1.5B parameters. The low-rank family lands near half its raw prediction,
a factor whose own scatter is about a quarter and which drifts with scale,
so it is a patch rather than a law. On decoded-reward Countdown training, a three-seed experiment
resolves no quality difference among full rank and ranks 1, 4, and 16 at equal
training samples, while rank 1 uses 41\% less steady-state time per update.
That task result is consistent with the NLL alignment evidence, not a claim
that the objectives are interchangeable. The measurement past the single-host boundary bears this out where it was
taken: on two A100 nodes the interconnect changes the contraction question
sharply, flipping the seed-regenerated arm from B to A across a $9.2$\,GiB/s
socket fabric (Section~\ref{subsec:boundary}). Whether that flip survives at
the faster InfiniBand of a production cluster is the question the single
socket point leaves open.

\paragraph{What to do differently.} On a single host with a fat
interconnect, contract locally and all-reduce (B) unless the perturbation
is low-rank at large model size, where gathering scalars and repeating the
contraction (A) is ahead by tens of percent. Keep fitness in float32:
bfloat16 collapses a population's losses to a handful of ties and
rank-based shaping of ties is noise. Do not force full matmul precision on
a low-rank run; it costs $1.6$--$1.9\times$ there and nearly nothing on the
dense arms. Above populations of about $10^4$, budget for the shaping
sort, which is replicated local compute, not communication.

\section{Conclusion}
The load-bearing beliefs about distributed evolution strategies were
measurable all along, and measured, they narrow. ES moves only scalars
under one of two contraction placements, and the placement that moves a
model-sized buffer every update wins much of the measured grid anyway;
where each wins moves with the accelerator. The low-rank rewrite's
GPU-shaped motivation strengthens rather than inverts on TPU. The only
synchronization point is the fitness gather, microseconds at every
measured cell; the shaping barrier above it is a replicated local sort
that reaches milliseconds only at extreme populations, and communication
is not the dominant cost in any measured cell.
On one 0.5B-parameter task, the fast perturbation family shows no
detectable quality loss over three seeds, the outcome the estimator
study's fitted curves predicted; and the folklore stability advantage
over one RL recipe is real at the learning-rate margin but concentrated
there, with a measured drift cost reported beside it. The instrument that
made these comparisons commensurable is released. The natural next
measurement is past the single-host boundary, where the contraction
question changes character because the interconnect does.

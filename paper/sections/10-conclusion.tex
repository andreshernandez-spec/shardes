\section{Conclusion}
The load-bearing beliefs about distributed evolution strategies were
measurable all along, and measured, they narrow. ES moves only scalars
under one of two contraction placements, and the placement that moves a
model-sized buffer every update wins much of the measured grid anyway;
where each wins moves with the accelerator, and both crossover signs recur
on the real model. The low-rank rewrite's GPU-shaped motivation strengthens
rather than inverts on TPU. The only synchronization point is the fitness
gather, microseconds at every measured cell; the shaping barrier above it is
a replicated local sort that reaches milliseconds only at extreme
populations, and communication is not the dominant cost in any measured
cell. On a 0.5B-parameter task, the fast perturbation family shows no
detectable quality loss over three seeds and reaches that quality with a
$1.7\times$ accelerator-time advantage. Against true gradients at 0.5B and
1.5B, the estimator law predicts full-rank cosine within 10\%, gets the
within-arm population scaling right, and transfers a 0.5B low-rank
calibration to 1.5B within 26\%, although the correction itself drifts with
scale. The folklore stability advantage over one RL recipe is real at the learning-rate
margin but concentrated there, with a measured drift cost reported beside
it. The instrument that made these comparisons commensurable is released.
The natural next measurement is past the single-host boundary, where the
contraction question changes character because the interconnect does.

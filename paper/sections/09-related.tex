\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Four threads locate the gap this paper fills. \emph{ES at scale}: \citet{salimans2017} made distributed ES
credible with scalar-only communication and fixed hyperparameters across
tasks; the 2025 pair \citep{qiu2025,eggroll2025} brought it to LLMs with the
opposite structural bets this paper measures; ARS \citep{mania2018} and deep neuroevolution
\citep{such2017,conti2017,chrabaszcz2018} are the simplicity-first
ancestors. \emph{Zeroth-order optimization for
LLMs}: the MeZO lineage \citep{malladi2023} and its successors optimize with
perturbations for
memory reasons rather than parallelism ones, a sibling motivation with
different systems consequences. \emph{Estimator theory}: Nesterov and
Spokoiny's smoothed-objective analysis \citep{nesterov2017} and its
dimension-dependence lower bounds \citep{duchi2015,berahas2022} are the
frame our accuracy measurements live in; the coupling and
quasi-Monte-Carlo literature promised variance reductions that our phase-0
study measured as absent in the relevant regime, a negative result recorded
rather than repeated. \emph{Systems}: JAX's shard\_map lineage is the
substrate \citep{jax2018,gspmd2021}; evosax \citep{lange2022evosax} is the incumbent library whose
flattening design this paper measures the consequences of, in Table~\ref{tab:tb1}'s failure row
rather than in prose.

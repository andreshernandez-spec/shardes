\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Four threads. \emph{ES at scale}: Salimans et al.\ made distributed ES
credible with scalar-only communication and fixed hyperparameters across
tasks; the 2025 pair (Qiu et al., EGGROLL) brought it to LLMs with the
opposite structural bets this paper measures; ARS and deep neuroevolution
are the simplicity-first ancestors. \emph{Zeroth-order optimization for
LLMs}: the MeZO lineage and its successors optimize with perturbations for
memory reasons rather than parallelism ones, a sibling motivation with
different systems consequences. \emph{Estimator theory}: Nesterov and
Spokoiny's smoothed-objective analysis and its dimension-dependence lower
bounds are the frame our accuracy measurements live in; the coupling and
quasi-Monte-Carlo literature promised variance reductions that our phase-0
study measured as absent in the relevant regime, a negative result recorded
rather than repeated. \emph{Systems}: JAX's shard\_map lineage is the
substrate; evosax is the incumbent library whose flattening design this
paper measures the consequences of, in Table~\ref{tab:tb1}'s failure row
rather than in prose.

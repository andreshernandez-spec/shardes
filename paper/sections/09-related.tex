\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Five threads locate the gap this paper fills. \emph{ES at scale}: \citet{salimans2017} made distributed ES
credible with scalar-only communication and fixed hyperparameters across
tasks; the 2025 pair \citep{qiu2025,eggroll2025} brought it to LLMs with the
opposite structural bets this paper measures; ARS \citep{mania2018} and deep neuroevolution
\citep{such2017,conti2017,chrabaszcz2018} are the simplicity-first
ancestors. \emph{Zeroth-order optimization for
LLMs}: the MeZO lineage \citep{malladi2023} and its successors optimize with
perturbations for
memory reasons rather than parallelism ones, a sibling motivation with
different systems consequences. \emph{Estimator theory}: Nesterov and
Spokoiny's smoothed-objective analysis \citep{nesterov2017} and its
dimension-dependence lower bounds \citep{duchi2015,berahas2022} are the
frame the accuracy measurements of Section~\ref{sec:endtoend} live in;
the coupling and quasi-Monte-Carlo literature promised variance
reductions that our estimator study measured as absent in the relevant
regime (Figure~\ref{fig:f5}), a negative result recorded rather than
repeated. \emph{RL stability}: the stabilization machinery ES omits
exists for stated reasons, from the divergence results behind target
networks \citep{baird1995,tsitsiklis1997,vanhasselt2018} to the step-size
limits behind trust regions and reference-model penalties
\citep{kakade2002,schulman2015trpo,ouyang2022}; large-scale empirical
studies question which parts bind in practice
\citep{engstrom2020,andrychowicz2020,orz2025}, and ES-side reports of
drift-induced forgetting \citep{abdi2026forgetting} are the cost side of
the same ledger. Section~\ref{sec:endtoend} measures both sides on one
task under one protocol. \emph{Systems}: JAX's shard\_map lineage is the
substrate \citep{jax2018,gspmd2021}; evosax \citep{lange2022evosax} is the incumbent library whose
flattening design this paper measures the consequences of, in Table~\ref{tab:tb1}'s failure row
rather than in prose.

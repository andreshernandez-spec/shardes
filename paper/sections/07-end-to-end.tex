\paragraph{Task quality across ranks.} At population $N{=}30$, held-out
performance is flat across full-rank and rank-\{1,4,16\} perturbations
(Figure~\ref{fig:f7}), and this tie is \emph{predicted}: the phase-0
estimator study's fitted curves put all ranks within noise of each other at
this population-to-effective-dimension ratio.

\paragraph{Determinism and invariance on real hardware.} For a fixed seed and
compiled program, the full training step is bitwise reproducible across
processes on an 8-GPU node, and the update computed on 1 and 8 devices agrees
to $6\times10^{-6}$ relative error under a fixed set of fitnesses. Trajectory
equality across \emph{different} compiled programs is explicitly not claimed:
greedy decoding flips near-ties under different compiler fusion choices, and
we document the boundary rather than promise across it.

\paragraph{The reference baseline, honestly.} GRPO with Qiu et al.'s published
hyperparameters, untuned in either direction: one of three seeds collapses,
two freeze early. We report it as seed variance in the reference, not as a
victory for ES; the figure's role is that both algorithm families run end to
end in comparable budgets, not a leaderboard.

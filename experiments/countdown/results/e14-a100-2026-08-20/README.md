# E14: stability under hyperparameter perturbation, 2026-08-20/21

24 runs on one rented A100 (pod 2a1683q0izmcd8, ~$32), code at 63bf81d, per the
design committed before the run (docs/08). Verdicts computed by
`analyze_e14.py`, figure by `plot_e14.py` (F8), metrics exactly as pre-defined.
The multiplier-1 columns are E13's committed curves read at the same 150-step
horizon. One infrastructure incident, recorded: the first campaign script filled
the 80 GB disk with GRPO optimizer checkpoints (17 GB per run) and lost two runs
to ENOSPC; the resumable v2 script deletes checkpoints after each held-out eval,
and the affected runs were rerun from scratch.

## Verdict counts (12 perturbed runs per algorithm)

- **ES: 0 collapsed, 3 frozen.** lr x8 trains at 0.145-0.150 (above its x1
  reference at this horizon); lr/8 slower but healthy; sigma/4 healthy with
  wider seed spread; sigma x4 freezes just above the base floor on all three
  seeds. The ES failure mode observed is stagnation, never destruction.
- **GRPO: 3 collapsed, 0 frozen.** lr x8 collapses to 0.000 on all three seeds,
  below even format compliance, entropy 0. lr/8 is healthy (0.142-0.181).

## The ablations came back fine, and that is reported at full volume

kl_beta=0 (anchor removed): 0.100-0.144. clip widened to never bind:
0.100-0.144. Both comparable to the x1 reference at this horizon. The
falsification list in docs/08 said "the kl_beta=0 arm training fine" would kill
that sub-claim of C7, and it did: on a rule-based verifier (nothing for the
policy to hack, unlike the learned/proxy rewards where the anchor earns its
keep) at a 150-step horizon, neither single stabilizer is individually
load-bearing. This is consistent with Engstrom et al. (PPO-NoClip) and with
Open-Reasoner-Zero's no-KL result, and it sharpens C7 rather than rescuing it:

**C7 as measured: the learning rate is where critic-free GRPO is catastrophically
fragile (x8 of the published value takes every seed to zero), while ES crosses
the same 64x range without a collapse; ES's one observed failure (sigma x4) is a
freeze at the base model, not a loss of it.**

## The drift row, because the counter-literature earned it

param_l2_from_init at the final checkpoint: ES at its own published settings
moves ~6.8 (es-x1, three runs, 2026-08-21, whose finals 0.150-0.156 also
independently reproduce E13's x1 arm); lr x8 moves ~50 and lr/8 ~1; healthy
GRPO runs move 0.3-2 and GRPO's collapsed runs ~10. Two honest sentences
follow: ES is a larger-movement method at baseline (~3-20x healthy GRPO), and
its lr x8 tolerance multiplies that by ~7. This is exactly what
arXiv:2604.01499 and arXiv:2601.20861 predict; anyone citing E14's stability
must also cite this row.

## Entropy

Every GRPO run's entropy is near-exhausted by step 150 (0.001-0.05; one beta-0
seed at 0.40), including the healthy ones, with the small drawdowns after peak
that the entropy-exchange account predicts (arXiv:2505.22617). Recorded in each
run's train-log.jsonl (the full TRL log history).

#
# Two things to read this for.
#
# 1. lowrank_r1 distributes. full falls to 0.27 at D=8 and evalFLOP to 0.125, which is the
#    ideal 1/8 exactly. The gap between 0.27 and 0.125 is the dispatch floor, which GROWS
#    7.3x to 7.7x from D=1 to D=8 (0.18 ms to 1.45 ms). That is what caps efficiency at
#    these shapes, not communication and not the contraction.
#
# 2. seed_regenerated does not, and its B row is the clearest statement of why. tell falls
#    to 0.15, near the ideal, because contraction.contract_sharded runs its scan under
#    shard_map. eval stays at 1.02 because ShardedES.apply did not. The contraction was
#    taught to shard a scan and the evaluation was not.
#    Fixed after this table was measured; see docs/diagnosis-seed-regenerated-scan.md.
#
# Two cautions about the columns.
#
# evalFLOP IS NOT EVIDENCE FOR THE SCAN STRATEGIES. XLA lowers lax.scan to a while loop and
# cost_analysis counts the body once, ignoring the trip count, so seed_regenerated reads
# 1.000 whether or not the scan is partitioned. Measured at d_model=128, n=64: 8 members and
# 64 members both report ~10.31 MFLOP. The evidence that seed_regenerated does not
# distribute is the WALL CLOCK below, flat at 431.95 to 440.35 ms across D=1,2,4,8.
# evalFLOP remains valid for the vmap strategies.
#
# eval, tell and shaping are measured BY DIFFERENCE, so a part smaller than the noise on the
# whole comes out negative. lowrank_r1/A at D=8 reads tell -1.40 and shaping -3.11. Those
# cells are not measurements of anything and should not be quoted.
#
